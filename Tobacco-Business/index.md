# Tobacco Business

Aldi's family business: tobacco grading, run with his dad, in partnership with **Gudang
Garam since 1988**. Season is ~3 months a year; the other ~9 months are free for other work
(including [[A-Brain-Project-Context|KPM development]]). His dad has no employees due to
financial difficulty, which is why Aldi is needed during season rather than free to choose
other work then — see [[Personal-Context]] for the fuller picture.

is a starting suggestion, not fixed; adjust as real material comes in.
