Sound studio · round 2

Capybara: 8-bit voices, made the way Game Boy and NES games make them - a short beep from a square or triangle wave, played at 4 pitches while the line types out. Intro: a typing tick for each letter of the two text lines, and invented sounds for the dots: the ring, the dots flying into your name, the moment the name lands, and the name leaving.

Each card starts on my pick and Today's sound is there to compare. Press the gold button to hear it in its moment; letting go of a slider plays the result. When it sounds right, press Save.
