Three ways the terminal arrives

This is the panel's entrance and exit — the phone's answer to the PC book flying out of its slot and opening. Press the tutorial chip in a phone below; press the × on the pad's status strip to send it back.

All three animate the same terminal out of the same parts list — the housing, the gold rim, the edge meter, the rail lamps, the picture, the text — so what you are comparing is the choreography and nothing else. Exit is faster than entry in all three. Phone only; the PC keeps the book.
