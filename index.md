# A-Brain — Vault Index

*"The power comes from the map, not the arbitrary folders."* Start here in any new session
before digging into a specific folder.

## Read first, always

1. **[[Personal-Context]]** — who Aldi is, what he's building toward, how he wants to work.
   The single highest-leverage file in the vault.
2. **[[CLAUDE.md]]** — the maintainer rulebook. Currently governs the KPM `Wiki/` layer in
   detail; has a scope note (2026-07-27) covering the rest of the vault at a lighter level.
3. **[[PLAN-A-Brain-agentic-OS]]** — the living plan for this whole system: what's built, what
   isn't, and why, with a dated revision log. Check this before assuming what stage a piece of
   work is at.

- **`Wiki/`** — KPM app knowledge (concepts, entities, decisions). Start at
  [[Wiki/Index|Wiki/Index.md]] (AI-facing catalog) or [[Wiki/MOC|Wiki/MOC.md]] (human browsing
  map).
- **[[Tobacco-Business/index|Tobacco-Business/]]** — the family tobacco-grading business.
  Scaffolding only as of 2026-07-27, no real content ingested yet.
- **[[Crypto-Learning/index|Crypto-Learning/]]** — crypto/financial learning. Scaffolding
  only, no real content ingested yet.
- **[[Personal/index|Personal/]]** — life goals and reflection beyond any single project.
  [[Personal-Context]] itself lives at vault root, not inside this folder.
- **[[graphify/index|graphify/]]** — Graphify's auto-generated code-graph export for KPM
  (and future projects). Scaffolding only as of 2026-07-27, nothing exported yet.
