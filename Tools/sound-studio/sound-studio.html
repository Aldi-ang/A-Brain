Capybara: try Agent (firm) - a low, serious, focused voice that still moves: each time he talks, the number of syllables changes, he speeds up and slows down like a person, one word gets the stress, and a sentence settles into a low creak at its end. Watch him in his corner, the way the app shows him, saying his real lines. Play a conversation plays five lines in a row - one of them twice, so you can hear it is never the same: each line gets its own mood (calm, cheerful, curious, excited) and a fresh roll of the dice. The Variety slider sets how different. Intro: your pick is saved - it is below if you want to hear it again.

Each card starts on my pick and Today's sound is there to compare. Press the gold button to hear it in its moment; letting go of a slider plays the result. When it sounds right, press Save.
