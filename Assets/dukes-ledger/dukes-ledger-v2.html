What changed from v1

+ and − now sit on the row and step the quantity by one. Tapping the product panel opens the quantity drawer with an animation instead. C then Done removes the line from the manifest entirely. Search is back at the top. Tone is locked to parchment.

Why it kept snapping back

Not the scroll code. When the quantity panel was open, its input held keyboard focus, and every background re-render — the merchant's speech bubble clearing, the undo toast expiring — re-applied that focus. Focusing an element scrolls it into view, so the list was yanked back to wherever the panel sat: top if the panel was at the top, bottom if it was at the bottom. Exactly what you described in both directions.

Fixed with focus({preventScroll:true}), and the scroll position is now restored after focus rather than before, so nothing can override it.

Sliding, not snapping

Panels travel on a 340 ms ease; small feedback stays at 150 ms so it still feels instant. The quantity panel now animates closed too, instead of vanishing. Lite Mode keeps the snap — zero duration — because that is the honest cheap-phone behaviour, not a downgrade.

Drag the manifest — flicker fixed

Drag the grip up or down; it snaps between closed, half and full. Tapping the bar still toggles it.

The bug you hit was real. The merchant's speech bubble clears itself on a timer, and that timer rebuilt the whole screen. Start dragging inside that window and the panel was destroyed mid-gesture, so it jumped back and the leftover tap landed on a product row underneath. Timers now wait until the drag ends, and the stray tap is swallowed.

Geofence, customer search, auto-detect

Open the manifest and use the GPS fix control to move yourself around. At store auto-selects the nearest customer inside the 50 m fence and tags it AUTO-DETECTED. Too far blocks the sale and offers the 100 m HQ bypass — request it, send the storefront photo, and HQ answers a moment later. Locking is the waiting state; commit stays disabled throughout.

The customer box is a live search over the profiled list, sorted nearest-first with each distance shown. Type a name that does not match and it becomes a walk-in.

Retur mode is back

Switch to Retur inside the manifest. Buyback and Exchange both work, Exchange forces the price to zero and marks the line damaged, the damage reason lives in the same drawer as the quantity, and a line can be marked IOU instead of handed over now.
