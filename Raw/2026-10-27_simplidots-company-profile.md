# SimpliDOTS company profile - read 2026-10-27

Source: his file `Desktop/my stuff/RESEARCH/SimpliDOTS - Company Profile.pdf` (48 pages, PT Simpli Inovatif Mandiri,
Jakarta, BEI tower). Text read with pdf.js; a summary, not a copy. Context: [[2026-10-24_pricing-and-b2b-engineering-business]].

## The facts
- **No prices anywhere in the deck.** The quote is still the only way (his to ask).
- Size claims: 600+ companies, 7.000+ users (6.000+ on another page), 1.000.000+ retailers, 30+ provinces, CSAT 86,27%.
  Started 2017 in Medan as IT consultants; SaaS since 2019.
- Products: SFA app (taking order + canvas), Supervision app, Sales Management Hub (office web), Smart Portal (the
  factory/principal sees its distributors' data, consolidated), Delivery & Route Optimization with e-signature proof of
  delivery, a Retail app for shops to order themselves, BI dashboards, API/export bridges to 30+ ERPs.
- Case studies: Arata Jaya Mandiri (AICE distributor, Bandung, 125 salesmen) "16%" growth, "2-4x" faster distribution;
  Wahana Mekar Lestari (Khong Guan distributor, 30+ salesmen) "30-50%" sales growth a month for 6 months; Sinar Mas
  (230 salesmen at 50 distributors), Kalbe (130-150 motorists), Charoen Pokphand (200+ sales, "active in one week").
  All are their own claims, not audited.

## Where KPM already stands level
Canvas selling, offline mode with sync, GPS check-in on a route plan, live map of the team, IOU / receivables, promo
giving + factory bundles, end-of-day, tiers with region locks.

## Gaps worth a decision (not built, nothing promised)
1. **Promo quota** - their SFA: "bonus barang berdasarkan kuota". A cap per offer (e.g. 50 T-shirts for Bandung this
   month). Fits the team offers: it stops a regional admin burning the boss's promo stock.
2. **Promo effectiveness** - their pitch to the factory: measure whether a promo worked. His "as long as sales
   increasing" is exactly this; KPM cannot answer it yet (a sale does not remember which offer filled it).
3. **Effective call** - visits that ended in an order, per salesman, ranked. Their Supervision KPI set: visits, effective
   call, orders without a visit, target per person.
4. **Customer insight on the visit** - debt + due date, favourite products, visit history, on one card.
5. **Principal portal** - the factory sees its distributors' sales. The selling point to Cello/Tawang if they sell
   through distributors.
6. **Proof of delivery with a signature** - only if he delivers (taking order), not for canvas.

## What it means for selling KPM
They sell to distributors AND factories, with a week-long rollout and a team behind them. KPM's edge for a class 3
factory is price, his own support, and features made for kretek (Bal/Slop/Bks, pita cukai, canvas vans). Their weak
spot for a small factory: quote-only pricing and an enterprise sales process.
