Four ways in. One way out.

You took Deploy's closing, so the exit is settled and every study below uses it: the picture squeezes to a scanline, the slab folds to a bar, and the bar snaps back to the chip. What changes between the four is only the way in.

Locked

The exit is Deploy's, unchanged. Press the × on any pad's status strip and all four leave the same way — 380 ms, faster than any of the entrances, because by then you have already decided to go.

All four move the same six parts — the housing, the gold rim, the edge meter, the rail lamps, the picture, the text. What differs is which part carries the arrival, and in what order. The two numbers under each name are when the slab is on screen and when the last word has finished writing.
