# I am pricing a B2B mobile + web app for a small Indonesian cigarette (kretek) fa

**Question:** I am pricing a B2B mobile + web app for a small Indonesian cigarette (kretek) factory, excise class "Golongan III" (small producer, roughly 20-60 field users). The app covers: master warehouse stock, shipments to regional warehouses, loading stock onto each salesman's van/motorbike, the sales terminal and printed receipt at the shop, consignment (goods left at shops, paid later), end-of-day cash and stock reconciliation, stock counts, returns and damaged-goods quarantine, GPS maps and journey plans. In industry terms it is sales force automation (SFA) + van sales + a light distributor management system (DMS) for FMCG.

Please research the peer-reviewed and academic literature and answer, with citations:
1. What prices (per user per month, or per company per month, or implementation fees) are reported for sales force automation, van sales, mobile ordering, distributor management or similar CRM/field-sales software used by small and medium enterprises or FMCG/consumer-goods distributors - especially in Indonesia, Southeast Asia, India or other emerging markets. Report any concrete numbers with currency and year.
2. What the evidence says about the measurable benefit of such systems for distributors/field sales (sales uplift, stock shrinkage or leakage reduction, cash reconciliation errors, productivity), with numbers where available - these set a value-based price ceiling.
3. What the literature recommends for pricing B2B SaaS to SMEs in emerging markets: per-user vs flat vs tiered, value-based pricing, penetration pricing for a first customer/pilot, implementation/onboarding fees, and what drives SME willingness to pay.
4. Any studies on technology adoption barriers for small Indonesian manufacturers or distributors (cost sensitivity, trust in a small vendor), since the seller is a one-person developer selling his first app.
Output: a concise synthesis with a table of every concrete price or benefit number found (source, year, country, number), then pricing recommendations the evidence supports, and clearly say where the literature has no number.

## Benefits and Value Ceiling

Evidence across field-sales, mobile CRM, inventory-control, and Indonesian FMCG distribution studies consistently says these systems improve **speed, accuracy, visibility, and control**, but the supplied papers rarely quantify gains in sales uplift, shrinkage reduction, or reconciliation-error reduction with hard percentages [[3.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-impact-of-mobile-customer-relationship-management-rodriguez-boyer/500b263263965bb6abadc7ecbe3d8cca/) [[3.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-impact-of-mobile-customer-relationship-management-rodriguez-boyer/500b263263965bb6abadc7ecbe3d8cca/) [[9.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/a-cloudbased-sales-and-stock-control-system-for-onah-onuoha/566105b6abfb5751a1c207be43e35d3b/) [[10.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/kao-product-sales-accounting-information-system-at-pt-maulana-indarriyanti/4f21d12b64475d4386cd518df8f4d372/).

- **Real-time stock and sales visibility** is the clearest repeated benefit, supporting better inventory allocation and preventing sales against unavailable stock [[11.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/improving-salesperson-effectiveness-through-sales-qehaja-kutllovci/b0d089a5f896513fb8fc0d94e4685301/) [[10.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/kao-product-sales-accounting-information-system-at-pt-maulana-indarriyanti/4f21d12b64475d4386cd518df8f4d372/) [[9.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/a-cloudbased-sales-and-stock-control-system-for-onah-onuoha/566105b6abfb5751a1c207be43e35d3b/).
- **Error reduction and reporting speed** recur across the evidence, especially versus paper or spreadsheet processes [[11.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/improving-salesperson-effectiveness-through-sales-qehaja-kutllovci/b0d089a5f896513fb8fc0d94e4685301/) [[10.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/kao-product-sales-accounting-information-system-at-pt-maulana-indarriyanti/4f21d12b64475d4386cd518df8f4d372/) [[10.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/kao-product-sales-accounting-information-system-at-pt-maulana-indarriyanti/4f21d12b64475d4386cd518df8f4d372/).
- **Fraud and leakage control** appears important in Indonesian FMCG distribution through locked pricing, automated reconciliation, and route controls, but the supplied studies give no percentage savings [[10.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/kao-product-sales-accounting-information-system-at-pt-maulana-indarriyanti/4f21d12b64475d4386cd518df8f4d372/) [[10.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/kao-product-sales-accounting-information-system-at-pt-maulana-indarriyanti/4f21d12b64475d4386cd518df8f4d372/) [[10.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/kao-product-sales-accounting-information-system-at-pt-maulana-indarriyanti/4f21d12b64475d4386cd518df8f4d372/).

Because the literature provides **almost no hard ROI percentages** for this exact use case, a defensible value ceiling should be tied to a customer’s avoided leakage, admin labor saved, and incremental route productivity rather than to published benchmarks. The strongest empirical support is that digital adoption improves SME performance, and that mobile CRM/SFA improves collaboration and sales-process effectiveness when embedded in workflow, not used as a standalone app [[6.14]](https://consensus.app/papers/pursuing-longterm-business-performance-investigating-kurniasari-lestari/89494409122b5362922b942166767825/) [[3.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-impact-of-mobile-customer-relationship-management-rodriguez-boyer/500b263263965bb6abadc7ecbe3d8cca/) [[3.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-impact-of-mobile-customer-relationship-management-rodriguez-boyer/500b263263965bb6abadc7ecbe3d8cca/).

## Pricing Guidance

The SaaS pricing literature supports **subscription pricing**, usually with a **per-user fee** that is lower than perpetual-license equivalents, then layered with service-level or feature differentiation rather than a single one-size-fits-all plan [[12.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/revenue-models-and-pricing-strategies-in-the-b2b-saas-dempsey-kelliher/1cefe55882c15bdb884acce152264bcb/) [[1.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/bridging-the-stateoftheart-and-the-stateofthepractice-of-saltan-smolander/608d4a2221b3523890288ad099517fe4/) [[5.6]](https://consensus.app/papers/saas-pricing-model-transformation-from-perpetual-khandelwal/e2f4eeb7b3b856b7b042b5c3e79d7976/).

- **Per-user pricing** is standard in B2B SaaS and fits named sales reps, warehouse staff, and supervisors [[12.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/revenue-models-and-pricing-strategies-in-the-b2b-saas-dempsey-kelliher/1cefe55882c15bdb884acce152264bcb/).
- **Tiered packaging** is recommended because willingness to pay differs by segment, usage pattern, and realized value [[13.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/guardrailed-elasticity-pricing-a-churnaware-forecasting-sapru/f39103e581405109abc543630ad52996/) [[5.7]](https://consensus.app/papers/saas-pricing-model-transformation-from-perpetual-khandelwal/e2f4eeb7b3b856b7b042b5c3e79d7976/).
- For a **first customer**, the literature supports **penetration pricing** as a practical acquisition strategy when market knowledge is limited [[14.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/impact-of-pricing-schemes-on-a-market-for-rohitratana-altmann/63f2bd9a8d21582a91b54b879c39e6f6/) [[14.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/impact-of-pricing-schemes-on-a-market-for-rohitratana-altmann/63f2bd9a8d21582a91b54b879c39e6f6/).

For your case, the evidence best supports a **hybrid model**: a modest base subscription plus per-active-field-user pricing, with separate one-time onboarding for data setup, printer/device setup, training, and form customization. The supplied papers discuss pricing complexity, stakeholder input, value alignment, and customer education, but they do **not** provide empirical numeric norms for implementation fees or ideal tier cutoffs [[1.4]](https://consensus.app/papers/bridging-the-stateoftheart-and-the-stateofthepractice-of-saltan-smolander/608d4a2221b3523890288ad099517fe4/) [[1.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/bridging-the-stateoftheart-and-the-stateofthepractice-of-saltan-smolander/608d4a2221b3523890288ad099517fe4/) [[5.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/saas-pricing-model-transformation-from-perpetual-khandelwal/e2f4eeb7b3b856b7b042b5c3e79d7976/).

## Adoption Barriers

For Indonesian and other emerging-market SMEs, adoption barriers are dominated by **perceived cost/risk, limited expertise, weak readiness, and data-security concerns**, not by software features alone [[7.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/a-mixedmethod-study-on-the-barriers-of-industry-40-adoption-fernando-wahyuni-td/e6862097db6b510fa0d3b5cfba96991b/) [[8.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/digital-technology-adoption-in-smes-what-technological-nekmahmud-ebrahimi/14de0b4c56465bafb953d48dbc94452f/) [[15.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/factors-affecting-information-communication-technology-kumar-goel/13a51b8d5ce55b31aa4f4f2d8728f593/).

- In **Indonesian manufacturing SMEs**, key barriers were higher-risk investment, insecure data sharing, lack of expertise, lack of incentive, and unclear policy [[7.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/a-mixedmethod-study-on-the-barriers-of-industry-40-adoption-fernando-wahyuni-td/e6862097db6b510fa0d3b5cfba96991b/).
- In **Indonesian traditional-market SMEs**, digital adoption depends on financial accessibility, performance expectancy, effort expectancy, and social influence [[6.15]](https://consensus.app/papers/pursuing-longterm-business-performance-investigating-kurniasari-lestari/89494409122b5362922b942166767825/).
- Across emerging-country SMEs, **perceived cost**, management support, competitive pressure, and government support significantly shape ICT adoption [[8.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/digital-technology-adoption-in-smes-what-technological-nekmahmud-ebrahimi/14de0b4c56465bafb953d48dbc94452f/).

Trust in a small vendor is less directly supported than cost and capability barriers. One Malaysian manufacturer study found **dependency mattered but trust did not significantly influence adoption**, suggesting that if your app becomes operationally hard to replace, vendor size matters less than business usefulness [[16.1]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-role-of-technology-attributes-trust-and-dependency-on-abdullah/1df925ef727a5c2f8855d85266684450/) [[16.2]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-role-of-technology-attributes-trust-and-dependency-on-abdullah/1df925ef727a5c2f8855d85266684450/). Separate evidence still suggests that communication quality, knowledge transfer, and problem handling are sensitive relationship factors, so a solo developer should reduce buyer anxiety with strong onboarding, fast support, and a credible implementation plan [[16.3]](https://consensus.app/papers/the-role-of-technology-attributes-trust-and-dependency-on-abdullah/1df925ef727a5c2f8855d85266684450/) [[5.5]](https://consensus.app/papers/saas-pricing-model-transformation-from-perpetual-khandelwal/e2f4eeb7b3b856b7b042b5c3e79d7976/).

For a small Indonesian kretek producer, the literature supports pricing the app as a **low-risk subscription with tiered per-user scaling and a modest one-time onboarding fee**, while selling primarily on **error reduction, real-time stock control, and faster reconciliation**. The clearest gap is that the supplied academic literature provides **no concrete market price benchmarks** for SME SFA/van-sales/DMS software in Indonesia or comparable markets, and only sparse hard ROI percentages for this exact workflow.
