Journey Plan · a printable day

Day Log on paper

A salesman's day without watching the replay. Three ways to put it on an A4 sheet. The names, shops and amounts are made-up examples in the shape the app already keeps (time, shop, result, what was sold, the note).

A One salesman, one sheet my pick

His whole day as a ruled list, with the day's numbers on top and the shops he missed at the bottom. Space to sign it.

Printed from his Day Replay. "Print all" gives one sheet per salesman.

B The whole team, one sheet

Every salesman on one line: shops visited, sales, total, first and last visit, and a strip of his visits across the day.

Printed from the team list. Good for the evening check; no per-shop detail.

C One salesman, with his route

Like A, with a drawing of the order he walked the shops in. The list uses smaller type to make room, and the notes move under each shop.

The route is drawn from the shops' pins, not a map picture, so it prints clean.

Black ink on white so it copies and faxes well; gold only as a thin line under a sale. A sale row can carry the items sold, taken from its receipt.
