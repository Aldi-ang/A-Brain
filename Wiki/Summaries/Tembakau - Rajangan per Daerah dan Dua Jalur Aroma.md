# Tembakau - Rajangan per Daerah dan Dua Jalur Aroma

**Page (private artifact):** https://claude.ai/artifact/TFCFNSokovod18SEZaGfMY · source
`Tobacco-Business/Raw/2026-10-05_rajangan-per-daerah.html`.

## The ask, in his words
*"i just want to know why this year is only feel jedug but no or less aromatic when smoke, they may
smell good when smell naturally but no smell good when smoked"* - with bibit, nicotine, sugar and
smoking character for Muntilan/Prambanan, Temanggung and other famous rajangan regions.

## The answer: two paths of aroma
- Raw smell = compounds already in the leaf (terpenes, carotenoid breakdown products, "green,
  citrus"). Not destroyed by drought. Wu 2026 (Frontiers in Chemistry) traced leaf -> smoke: smoke
  aroma is dominated by furans and pyrazines made in pyrolysis; only 44% of neophytadiene reaches
  smoke; nicotine transfers ~30%.
- Smoke smell = made in the coal, mainly from sugar + amino acids via Amadori intermediates
  (Mitsui 2015; Visser 2026: sugars -> furans, pyranones). 2026 low sugar -> few precursors.
- Nicotine masks: alkaloids track impact and irritation, oppose aroma (Lin 2016).
- Fast-burning ngrapak strands: aroma compounds form best below 350 C and break above (Yan 2018,
  bio-oil pyrolysis - inference [likely]); drier cut tobacco gives more NH3 (Liu 2011).
- Less potassium under drought (Dong 2004) and K catalyses aroma-forming pyrolysis (Tong 2023)
  [likely].
