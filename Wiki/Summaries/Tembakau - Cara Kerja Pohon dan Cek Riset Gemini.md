## The plant as a factory (verified 2026-10-03)
| Part | Makes | What shifts it |
|---|---|---|
| Roots | nicotine (from N), sent up the stem, stored in leaf cells | dry soil concentrates it; more N raises it; topping raises root nicotine synthesis (Frontiers 2016) |
| Leaves | sugar, stored as starch | drought closes stomata: sugar down, nicotine up, ~5% avg sugar loss non-irrigated (Frontiers 2024, fpls.2024.1489993); ripening on the plant lowers protein, builds starch; imbon converts starch, burns sugar by respiration |
| Leaf position | lower = thin, light, fast imbon; upper (tenggok/protol) = thick, more nicotine and gum, slow imbon | his field words + Kentucky AGR-177: drought or excess-N leaf needs longer to mature |
| Glandular trichomes | sticky gum: diterpenes (cembranoids -> solanone), sucrose esters, and SOME alkaloids incl. nicotine; up to ~16% of leaf dry weight (Springer) | rain washes sucrose esters off (K326 study) - a dry year means less washing, not more loss |
| Top/flower | topping -> wound hormone -> more nicotine, thicker upper leaves; excess N grows more suckers (NC State) | |

## Gemini fact-check: 24 claims
5 true · 7 partly · 6 false · 2 no evidence · 4 do-not-do (was 4 / 3 before his definitions).

**His trade terms, his words (2026-10-03):** *"keluwasen is expired leaves, happens when imbon is
too long, ngrapak means when touched it is crisp feeling"*. He also corrected me: *"its not gemini
own meaning"* - I had written that Gemini made the definitions up. Gemini's meanings matched his;
the claim moved from "not checked" to "true". Lesson for the vault: a term that is not online is
his to define, but a matching definition elsewhere is not automatically invented.
Biggest errors: says 2026 leaf is LOW nicotine and HIGH sugar - drought research says the
opposite and so does his "jedug". It also contradicts itself (N-starved for nicotine, nitrate-rich
for ammonia; leaf too dry for enzymes yet wet enough to rot).
- Dad's claim: partly right - a little nicotine IS in trichome gum on the surface; Gemini's "never
  outside" is too absolute. But almost all nicotine is in the cells and sticky gum does not blow off.
- Wind/root lodging: real (Kitanosono & Osawa, J-STAGE), but mostly in WET, loose soil; 2026 soil
  was dry and hard.
- Aroma "evaporated in the field" and "wind snapped the trichomes": no evidence found.
- True: respiration burns sugar in a long imbon; colour and flavour are separate processes.
- Do not do: sugar spray to hide bitterness; fogger + re-imbon "so the factory won't see"
  (hiding material from the buyer); spice/alcohol/rose-water sauce (foreign material for factory
  buyers); wet-and-seal for months (mould, musty).
- His own idea in that chat (shade the widig 11-14 without moving the rajangan) = correct.

## Decision
Nothing owed. Keluwasen and ngrapak now defined by him (see above; also in page 1's glossary).
Still open: no wind data for Central Java 2026 found.

## Rejected on purpose
- Wind as a main 2026 cause (no evidence; dry hard soil argues against lodging).
- Any additive/saos advice for factory-bound rajangan.

Models applied: Inversion (which Gemini advice makes the lot worse), Second-Order Thinking (hidden
sugar -> grader finds it -> lot and supplier name pay next season).
