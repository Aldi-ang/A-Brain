What to build first so KPM beats Distri and SimpliDOTS

From our research (Oct 27-28), checked against the app today. Already done: seat limit, salesman sees only his own TODAY box.

MY PICK

A Salesman screens in Indonesian

KPM today

No language switch. Salesmen read "Search wares", "Manifest", "Pay bounty".

Rivals

Distri and SimpliDOTS are Indonesian from the first screen.

What it wins

20 salesmen understand it on day one. If they don't use it, Cello stops paying. Your screens stay English.

BIG JOB

B Effective call + target per salesman

KPM today

One company target only. Nobody sees "Budi visited 12 shops, only 4 ordered".

Rivals

Both sell it. Distri includes it from its Rp 75 rb-per-user plan.

What it wins

The boss who signs sees who works and who only visits. The data is already in KPM.

MEDIUM JOB

C Factory sees its distributors

KPM today

Each company sees only itself. Cello cannot see its distributors' sales.

Rivals

Both have it (SimpliDOTS "principal portal").

What it wins

KPM becomes a factory system, and each distributor's salesmen become paid seats.
