## His field claims, now with papers
- Urea -> leaves won't ripen in imbon: ammonium N delays yellowing (Gu 2026); excess N piles up
  chlorophyll -> green/mottled (Su 2026). YES.
- Early picking -> less aroma, less sugar: Xu 2008, Wei 2026, Chen 2019. YES.
- Upper leaves (tenggok/protol) take longer: Hu 2012, Zhang 2021, Zhang 2012 (+9 d harvest). YES.
- Sugar -> less anteb: acids lower smoke pH and free nicotine (Duell 2019, WHO/Talhout 2024,
  Talhout 2006). YES. "Sugar -> hotter": coal temperature never measured; added sugar raises
  irritant aldehydes (Baker 2003) - my inference that the "heat" is irritation.
- Bitter: nicotine adds bitterness and irritation (Pullicin 2019; Lin 2016). YES.

## Corrections to pages 1-2
1. Ngrapak: drier cut tobacco burns faster and gives more NH3 in smoke, but solid-phase combustion
   temperature did not change significantly (Liu 2011) - "panas" = fast burn + ammonia, not a
   hotter coal.
2. Dryness is not always bad: Madura tegal beats sawah (Zulfah 2025). The damage is water stress at
   the rapid-growth stage, which wrecked the sugar/nicotine ratio irreversibly (Gršić 2019).
3. Conflicts kept visible: Liao 2025 (less rain -> more sugar, Yunnan), Kurt 2021 (oriental improves
   under mild water stress).
