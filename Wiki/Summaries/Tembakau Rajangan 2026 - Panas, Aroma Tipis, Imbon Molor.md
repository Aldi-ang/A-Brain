## The problem, in his words
*"i want u to do research and visual representation for this research and do it fully make sure
that it is easier to understand for me and use indonesian language"* - about the 2026 rajangan
(grade B, Prambanan/Klaten): hot/nyegrak smoke, weak aroma, imbon stretched from 3 to 6 days.

## The answer (one chain)
Dry heat helped the harvest and jemur (bright colour, right moisture, price up) but hit EARLIER,
while leaves were growing (Jun-Aug): less sugar formed, nicotine/N concentrated, K/Ca/Mg uptake
down, thick force-ripened leaves. Then a slow imbon (6 days) burned more sugar by respiration.
- Hot smoke: burning sugar makes acid that tames nicotine; low sugar -> more alkaline smoke ->
  more free nicotine -> harsh (Leffingwell 1999). Over-dried rajangan burns faster and hotter.
- Thin aroma: sweet-savoury notes come from sugar x amino acid browning (Maillard) and carotenoid
  breakdown during ripening/imbon. "Oil lost" is only PARTLY true: drought does not reliably cut
  leaf oil (oriental tobacco is grown dry), and "berminyak" in the RSNI is a handling descriptor;
  grade B is never required to be oily.
- Slow imbon: thick N-rich leaves hold chlorophyll; very dry air makes the pile wilt, not ripen;
  some leaves picked unripe (hijau mati).

Own check: 26.34 mg CO2/min/kg at 27 °C -> ~2.6% dry matter/day; 61.87 at 43 °C -> ~6.1%/day.
Three extra imbon days ≈ 6-8% dry matter, mostly sugar and starch.

## Options for the lot at day 6 (and their cost)
| Option | Cost |
|---|---|
| Rajang now, jemur to "kering pasar", embun overnight, basket ageing, honest grade | Sugar already lost stays lost |
| Wait for more yellow | Burns more sugar (index falls after day 5) |
| Add sugar/sauce | Rejected at the warehouse (Djarum rule verified); supplier name damaged |
| Lab test 250 g per lot (sugar, nicotine, chloride, moisture) | Lab fee; the only thing that turns "likely" into "certain" |

## Rejected on purpose
- Chasing "oil" (sauce, sugar, extra N, delaying harvest): wrong medicine, can make it worse.
- Ethephon to speed yellowing: partial yellowing on unripe leaf; market demands pure tobacco.
- Blending with tobacco from another region: purity rule, warehouses reject it.
