Why 4 Modifications Make the Steamer Actually Work

  • Tilted Trunnion Cradle (+25° / -12°): Standing 179 cm upright is taller than your 171 cm height. Tilting on CoG trunnions puts loading at chest height and pours blended tobacco straight into a keranjang at -12°.
  • External Tire Bands + 4 Rollers: Supports 650 kg loaded drum externally so the thin ~2.5 mm steamer bottom does not tear off under bending load.
  • 4 Curved Helical Scoops (Zero Blades): Gentle waterfall cascade blends Temanggung / Madura / Boyolali evenly without breaking dry rajangan strands into bubuk dust.
  • Swing-Davit + 8 Toggle Clamps on Tupperware Lid: Keeps the EPDM U-gasket aroma/dust seal tight under tumbling load, while one worker opens the 48 kg lid cleanly.

Check Before Cutting Steel (Your Steamer Cylinder)

  • 1. Wall & Bottom Thickness: If cylinder wall is <2 mm thin galvanized sheet, bolt two external flat-bar rings (50×8 mm) first so trunnion rollers don't dent the shell.
  • 2. Steamer Doorway Clearance: Verify workshop doorway width ≥ 225 cm or make the twin A-frame stanchions bolted instead of welded solid to the skid so it fits through doors.
