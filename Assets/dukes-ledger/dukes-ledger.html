What the counter measures

On the current build, quantity and unit can only be edited inside the Manifest, and the Manifest is a separate full-screen tab. So every line costs a trip there and a trip back. A 12-line warung sale runs about 24 switches. The proposed design never needs one.

Two speeds, one control

Tap a row to add one. Tap the number badge to type an exact quantity and pick a unit — not a long-press, because nobody discovers a long-press. Small warung baskets stay one tap per line; a Grosir line of 40 Karton is three interactions instead of forty taps.

Karton is in the app already

The pricing math handles it — balsPerCarton || 4, exactly your 4 Bal — but the unit dropdown never offered it. Grosir customers currently cannot buy by Karton at all. The unit row here shows all four.

Landscape, on the current build

Switch orientation with Current build selected. Between 768 and 1023 pixels wide, both copies of the Manifest are hidden — no customer, no payment, no cart. That is today's behaviour, reproduced. The drawer design has no such band.

Grosir usually isn't cash

Since most new Grosir customers take Titip, payment and tempo days sit inside the expanded ledger where the sale ends, not buried below a scroll. Basket B lands there on purpose.

Merchant portrait is a placeholder silhouette — the real assets are 4.5 MB of local PNG and would not load here anyway. Sounds are synthesised in the browser, so you can judge the feel before anyone records a file.

Prices, stock and tier logic are representative, not live data. Plan: .claude/plans/merchantsales-dukes-ledger.md
