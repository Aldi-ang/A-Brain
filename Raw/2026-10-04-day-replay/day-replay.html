    Press any shop on the map to see what happened there, his note and the time; a sale has its receipt. Pressing a shop pauses the replay; "Play from here" carries on from that shop.

    Why the walk is drawn: a phone sends its position only when he sells or opens the app, so the line between two stops is the straight way from one to the next, not his real road.

    Where each line comes from in the app: a sale and an exchange are the terminal's own records (with their time). Stock full, competitor, request, closed and check are the Visit Report he sends from Journey Plan; the app already keeps each one with its time in the activity log. The time shown is when he pressed Report, not when he arrived.
